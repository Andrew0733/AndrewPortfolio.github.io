About Project

Food delivery, minus the friction.

Otlobly is a food delivery app concept designed for Cairo and the wider MENA market. The brief tackled the friction that makes ordering frustrating: forced sign-ups before browsing, fees revealed only at the last step, and checkout paths with too many taps.

The result is a bilingual (Arabic / English) mobile experience where the full price is visible from the first screen, guests can order without an account, and the whole flow takes four steps instead of seven — backed by a scalable design system built RTL-first.

Design Philosophy

Appetite-driven visuals. Trust-first pricing.

Large food photography does the selling, while the signature orange is reserved for one job: action. Neutrals keep the interface calm, clear badges build trust early, and the bilingual-first approach means RTL was designed in from day one — not patched in later.

From Insight to Decision

71% — hidden fees break trust→ Total visible at every step + fee breakdown in cart
68% — abandon when totals appear late→ Running total shown from Home, before checkout
58% — against forced sign-up→ Guest browsing & ordering, with optional Google login
52% — too many steps→ Sticky cart button + stepper directly in the cart
79% — want live tracking→ Tracking screen with ETA and driver status
57% — reorder the same meals→ Order History & one-tap Reorder in Profile
54% — prefer cash→ Cash on Delivery as the default payment option
63% — bilingual users→ RTL / LTR layouts with flexible, mirrored components
Ordering Journey

From craving to doorstep.

The typical delivery flow takes seven steps. Otlobly compresses it to four — with the full price on screen at every one of them.

01 ⌕

Browse

Discover restaurants as a guest — no account, no barrier. Badges show Free Delivery, ETA, and rating upfront.

02 ＋

Build

Add items with an in-cart stepper while the running total stays visible the whole time.

03 ✓

Confirm

Review the full price breakdown — items, fees, delivery — before placing the order. Cash on delivery by default.

04 ◉

Track

Follow the order live with ETA and driver status — removing the "where is my food?" anxiety.

App Screens

Every screen, with a reason.

Each screen below maps directly to a research insight — nothing was designed "just because it looks good."

01 · Home

Discover Without Signing Up

Restaurants, categories, and featured offers — with the running total pinned at the bottom from the very first screen.

02 · Menu

Appetite-Driven Browsing

Large food photography does the selling; orange is reserved for actions, and trust badges (Free Delivery · ETA · Rating) set expectations early.

03 · Cart

The Math, Out in the Open

An in-cart stepper and an itemized fee breakdown mean no surprises waiting at the end of checkout.

04 · Checkout

No Last-Step Surprises

Address, payment, and the full total on one screen — the direct answer to the 71% who resented hidden fees.

05 · Tracking

Live Order, Calmer User

ETA and driver status update in real time — the feature 79% of research participants called essential.

06 · Login / Sign Up

Optional, Not Forced

Email, phone, or Google — minimal fields, and entirely skippable. The 58% who resented forced registration can simply browse as guests.

07 · Profile

One Hub, One-Tap Reorder

Addresses, payments, favorites, and points — with Order History built for the 57% who reorder the same meals.
